Is RoundFair Available on Android?

RoundFair is available on the App Store only. There is no Android app and no web version of the app.

Which devices are supported?

RoundFair runs on iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later. The App Store also lists iPad models as compatible. Live sharing uses Apple's iCloud, which is one reason the app is built for Apple devices.

Using RoundFair in a group with Android phones

You do not need everyone to have the app. One person with an iPhone can log the rounds for the whole group, read out whose turn it is, and export a plain text summary to any group chat at the end. Friends on Android see exactly who owes what.

Free tools that work on any phone

The browser tools on this site work on Android too: the who pays wheel, the round cost calculator, the receipt splitter and the bill split calculator.

Cross-platform alternatives

If your group needs an app on both iPhone and Android, a shared expense ledger may suit you better. Our Tricount alternatives page lists options, including apps available on Android.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an Android version of RoundFair?

No. RoundFair is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.

Can friends with Android phones still see the results?

Yes. Export the plain text summary and share it in your group chat.
