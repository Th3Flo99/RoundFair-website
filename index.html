FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before your first round.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your personal iCloud. RoundFair has no servers and no access to your data.

How does group sharing work?

Sessions can be shared through iCloud (CloudKit); everyone needs an iCloud account on their device.

Does RoundFair cost anything?

RoundFair is free to download on the App Store. Open it and start a session, no purchase required to get going.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. RoundFair is offline-first and fully usable without a connection. iCloud sharing is optional and syncs when you're back online.

Which currencies are supported?

Every session has its own currency. Pick it when you create the session, all amounts are shown in that currency.

Can I share the overview with the group?

Yes. Export the session as plain text with participants, rounds and balances, and share it through any app.

What are taunt notifications?

Optional, playful reminders during the session for whoever hasn't paid in a while. They are scheduled on your device and can be turned off in settings.
