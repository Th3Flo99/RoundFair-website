RoundFair features

Made by FairLead

RoundFair is a free iOS app that keeps a night out fair. Here is what each feature does and when you will use it.

Turn order

RoundFair tracks whose turn it is to buy the next round and who is behind, so the group never argues about it again. Free on iOS.

Saved spots

Save the bars and restaurants you visit often in RoundFair so starting the next night out takes a few taps. Free iOS app.

Restaurant mode

Split a restaurant bill by item with tip and tax in RoundFair. Itemized rounds make sure everyone pays for what they ordered. Free on iOS.

Split the bill

Split one total evenly among the people you choose with RoundFair: a taxi, dinner or hotel room, in a few taps. Free iOS app.

Who drank what

See who had what and the cost per person with RoundFair's drink categories and consumption overview, so every round is easy to check. Free iOS app.

Export the summary

Share a clear plain text overview of rounds and balances with your group using RoundFair's export, ready to paste anywhere. Free iOS app.

Live sharing and offline

Share a RoundFair session live with your group through iCloud. It works offline too, with no accounts and no servers to set up.

How the features fit together

A night with RoundFair has three stages. At the start, you create a session, add the group and pick a saved spot if it is somewhere you go often. During the night, you log each round in one tap, and turn order keeps track of who is up next and who is behind. At the end, you split the bill, check who drank what if anyone asks, and export the summary to the group chat.

For a sit-down meal, restaurant mode works with items instead of rounds, so each person pays for what they ordered plus a fair share of tip and tax. Live sharing keeps everyone's iPhone in sync through iCloud, and the app keeps working when the bar has no signal.

Built for nights out, not shared ledgers

Most bill splitting apps are ledgers: they record who paid for what over weeks or months and keep a running balance. That suits housemates and long trips. RoundFair is built for the night itself: whose round it is right now, who is behind, and a clean settlement before everyone goes home. To compare the two approaches, see our guide to the best bill splitting apps.

Private by design

RoundFair has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. Sessions are stored on your device and in your personal iCloud, and sharing uses Apple's CloudKit, so your group's data never passes through a RoundFair account. Read the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is RoundFair free?

Yes. RoundFair is free to download on the App Store. Open it, create a session and start tracking rounds straight away.

Does everyone in the group need the app?

No. One person can track the whole night and export the summary for the group. Friends who also have the app can follow the session live on their own iPhone.

Is RoundFair available on Android?

No. RoundFair is available for iPhone only.

Does RoundFair work without internet?

Yes. RoundFair works offline, so you can add rounds, see whose turn it is and settle up without a connection. iCloud sharing syncs when your phone is back online.
