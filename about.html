About RoundFair

Made by FairLead

RoundFair keeps a night out fair, without the maths or the awkwardness.

RoundFair is a free iOS app that tracks whose turn it is to buy the next round on a night out, and splits the bill fairly at the end.

What it does

  • Shows who is up now and who is behind.
  • Records rounds in one tap, with locations.
  • Shows who drank what, and the cost per person.
  • Splits the bill when you're ready, and exports the overview as plain text.
  • Shares live with the group through iCloud, and works offline.

Private by design

RoundFair has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. Your data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud. Read the privacy policy.
