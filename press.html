In one line

RoundFair is a free iOS app that tracks whose turn it is to buy the next round on a night out, and splits the bill fairly at the end.

Short description

RoundFair keeps a night out fair without the maths or the awkwardness. Friends add the group to a session and log each round in one tap; the app shows who is up now and who is behind. When the night is over, RoundFair works out everyone's balance and who should pay whom, including itemised restaurant bills with tip and tax. Sessions can be shared live through iCloud, the app works offline, and it has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Facts

  • Made by FairLead, founded by Florian Ronsse.
  • Free on the App Store, designed for iPhone (iOS 17 or later). iPhone only: no iPad, Android or web version.
  • First released on the App Store in July 2026; version 2.2 released in September 2026.
  • App Store category: Finance.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Data stays on the device and in the user's iCloud.
  • Available in Dutch, English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

What the app does

  • Create a session and add your friends.
  • Record who paid and who joined each round, and see whose turn it is next.
  • Split itemised bills, including tips and taxes.
  • See everyone's balance and who should pay whom.
  • Repeat a previous round without entering everything again.
  • Save venues with photos and remembered drink prices.
  • Share sessions with the group through iCloud, and export a plain text summary.
  • Spin the wheel to choose who buys the next round.

Each feature is explained on the features page. For how RoundFair differs from expense ledgers such as Tricount and Splitwise, see our comparisons.
