RoundFair use cases

Made by FairLead

Wherever a group shares costs, someone ends up keeping score. Here is how RoundFair helps in the situations that come up most.

Pub rounds with friends

Never argue over whose round it is again. RoundFair tracks pub rounds and shows who is next and who is behind. Free on iOS.

Bachelor and hen parties

Keep the weekend fair on a bachelor or hen party. Track rounds, share costs and cover the guest of honour with RoundFair.

Group trips

Track shared costs on a group trip with RoundFair: hotel, taxis, dinners and rounds, with a clear summary at the end. Free iOS app.

Restaurant dinners

Split a dinner bill by item with tip and tax so everyone pays for what they ordered. Try RoundFair, free on iOS, at your next dinner.

Work drinks and team nights

Keep team drinks and work nights out fair with RoundFair. Track rounds and split the bill without awkward conversations.

Which use case fits your group?

  • Pub rounds: friends taking turns to buy drinks, where the question all night is whose round it is.
  • Work drinks: people arrive and leave at different times, and nobody wants to talk money with colleagues.
  • Restaurant dinners: one bill, very different orders, and tip and tax on top.
  • Group trips: taxis, dinners and hotel rooms shared between different people over several days.
  • Bachelor and hen parties: a big group, a full schedule and a guest of honour whose share the others cover.

One app for the whole night

Real nights out mix these situations. Dinner turns into rounds at a bar, and a taxi home is shared by only half the group. RoundFair handles all of it in one session: rounds for the bar, restaurant mode for the meal and split the bill for anything else, with a single settlement at the end.

Not sure how to split fairly in the first place? Our guide to bill split methods explains when an even split works and when it does not, and the bill split calculator handles one-off bills in the browser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use RoundFair for things other than drinks?

Yes. Restaurant mode splits itemised meals with tip and tax, and split the bill divides any total, such as a taxi or hotel room, between the people who shared it.

Can the group change during the night?

Yes. People can join or leave a session and the turn order adjusts.

What if only some people shared a cost?

Choose who shared it when you add it. A taxi split by the four people who rode in it is not charged to the rest of the group.
