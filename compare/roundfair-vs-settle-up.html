RoundFair vs Settle Up: Which Fits Your Group?

Checked against official listings on 11 October 2026 · By Florian Ronsse, founder of FairLead

Settle Up is a cross-platform group expense app with a free tier and a Premium upgrade. RoundFair is a free iPhone app for rounds and nights out. Here is how they differ.

The short answer

Settle Up keeps track of a group's expenses and calculates the fewest transfers to settle them, on iOS, Android and the web. RoundFair focuses on rounds: who paid, who joined, whose turn is next. If your group is mixed iPhone and Android and shares costs over time, Settle Up is the practical choice. If you mainly need to keep a night of rounds fair, RoundFair is simpler.

When Settle Up is the better choice

  • Groups with Android users: RoundFair is iPhone and iPad only.
  • Long-running groups such as flatmates, with recurring expenses.
  • Groups that want reminders and nudges to settle debts.

When RoundFair is the better choice

  • A night out where rounds rotate and the question is who buys next.
  • People who want no accounts, no ads and no analytics: RoundFair keeps data on your device and in your personal iCloud.
  • A quick, one-evening session rather than an ongoing group.

Frequently asked questions

Is Settle Up free?

Its App Store listing describes a free app with an optional Premium upgrade that removes ads and adds features.

Can Android users join RoundFair?

No. RoundFair is only available on iPhone and iPad. One person can still track the night and share the plain text summary with everyone.
