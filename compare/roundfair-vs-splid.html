RoundFair vs Splid: Night-Out Rounds or Group Expenses?

Checked against official listings on 11 October 2026 · By Florian Ronsse, founder of FairLead

Splid and RoundFair both work without sign-up and both work offline, so they are easy to confuse. They solve different problems.

The short answer

Splid is an expense splitter: you enter the expenses of a holiday, a household or a relationship and it shows who owes how much to whom. RoundFair tracks rounds as they happen on a night out, shows whose turn it is and settles the evening. If your group shares costs over weeks, Splid fits. If your group takes turns at the bar, RoundFair fits.

When Splid is the better choice

  • Holidays where costs are in several currencies.
  • Households and couples who log expenses over a long period.
  • Groups that want PDF or spreadsheet exports.

When RoundFair is the better choice

  • Nights out where the question is whose round it is right now.
  • Itemised restaurant bills with tip and tax, followed by drinks, in one session.
  • Groups that want a spin wheel to pick the next buyer, and saved venues with remembered drink prices.

Frequently asked questions

Do Splid and RoundFair both work without an account?

Yes, according to Splid's App Store listing it needs no sign-up, and RoundFair has no accounts at all.

Which one handles foreign currencies?

Splid's listing describes automatic currency conversion. RoundFair records amounts without converting them.
